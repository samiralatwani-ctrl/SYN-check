## What the app does
It compares what is printed on a cosmetic or aesthetic-medical pack with published official sources. A match does not prove authenticity.

## Data we process
| Data | When | Why | Where | Kept for |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Barcode number | When you scan | Look-up in the reference database | Our server (Supabase, Singapore region) | Not stored unless you send a report |
| Label photo | When you choose to read a label | AI reading of the printed text and a pack-shape fingerprint | Sent to our server and to Anthropic (the reading model provider) | **Not stored by us.** Processed in real time and discarded; Anthropic processes it under its commercial terms |
| Random anonymous ID | First label reading or first report | Daily reading limit; link your report to your device | Our server | Until you delete your data |
| Usage log (time, success or error) | Each label reading | Daily limit and cost control | Our server | 30 days |
| Check and report (product, batch, dates, result, message text, purchase channel) | Only when you send a report | Review reports; improve the reference database | Our server | 24 months, or until you delete it |
| Seller details (optional) | If you type them in a report | Internal review only | Our server | As the report. **Never put in the message or published** |

We do **not** ask for your name, e-mail, phone number or location. There are **no** ads and no tracking, and we do not sell or share data for marketing.

## Report messages
A report you send by e-mail or WhatsApp leaves from your own mail or WhatsApp app, to the recipient you choose.

## Your rights
- **Delete your data:** Settings → "Delete my data from the server". Your checks, reports and usage log are deleted immediately.
- **Questions or corrections:** contact the support e-mail.

## Children
The app is not directed at children under 13.
